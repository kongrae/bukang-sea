# Skin artwork v2 — built-in ImageGen

2026-10-03. References: `../source/shark-portrait.png`, `../source/shark-swim.png`. Each atlas contains portrait (left) and overhead swim (right). Original generated alpha is preserved.

## sakura

Use case: precise-object-edit. Production character skin atlas for the existing Korean mobile puzzle game Shark SOS. Edit target 1 is the cheerful 3D shark portrait; edit target 2 is its strict overhead swimming sprite. Preserve the SAME recognizable round shark, face, eye shape, plump proportions, one forked tail, two pectoral fins, original head-right / tail-left orientation and camera for EACH view. Upgrade into a premium collectible skin with integrated sculpted details, tasteful material contrast and readable large motifs; decoration must follow the curved body and have real shading, never flat icons pasted over the face. Ivory muzzle, ivory belly, white eyes and dark pupils remain natural, not globally tinted.
Output ONE transparent RGBA atlas, landscape 2048x1024, exactly TWO equal square cells side by side. LEFT cell: portrait using reference 1 camera, RIGHT cell: strict 90-degree overhead using reference 2 camera. Both are the SAME costume seen from different cameras. One full shark per cell. Each whole silhouette fits inside its cell with 7% transparent horizontal padding; centered vertically. No overlap or objects crossing between cells. Head exactly right in both. No labels, lettering, grid, border, UI, ground shadow, bubbles or scenery. True transparent background everywhere outside shark. Smooth polished toy-like 3D, beautiful soft upper-left lighting, restrained microtexture; crisp uncluttered silhouette readable at 48px. Keep ornament away from eyes and outermost tail tips. Do not add legs, human hands, rings surrounding the body, or a crown unless specified by the theme.
THEME: SAKURA / cherry blossom. Warm blush-pink back, ivory underbelly, raspberry fin edges. A carefully sculpted asymmetric cluster of three pale sakura blossoms with layered five petals and golden stamens hugs the BASE of the dorsal fin, with a tiny jade leaf. Petal-shaped lighter pink markings flow toward the tail; a subtle raspberry ribbon wraps the fin base, not the face. One prominent flower visible from both cameras; refined soft pearlescent finish. No floating petals.

# 입체 시점 비교용 상어 · v1

2026-10-14, built-in `image_gen`으로 생성. 기본·등대(비교 화면에서는 항해사) 스킨의 투명 2×2 방향별 원본 2장과 WebP 8장이다. 정식 스킨 자산은 교체하지 않는다.

- 기준: `../shark-portrait.webp`, `../shark-swim.webp`, `../skins-v2/lighthouse-portrait.webp`, `../skins-v2/lighthouse-swim.webp`.
- `source/basic.png`, `source/lighthouse.png`: 생성 원본과 알파 보존. 순서 R / L / D / U.
- 각 방향은 독립된 그림이며 이미지를 회전해서 방향을 만들지 않는다. 약 50도 고정 카메라를 요청했다. 실제 이미지는 좌우에서 얼굴을 강조한 스타일화된 결과다.
- `tools/prepare-oblique-art.js <sharp module>`: 사분면의 알파 경계에서 크롭, 비율 유지, 최대 256px WebP. `crops.json`에 경계 보관.
- 게임판에는 수평 방향 최대 1.03칸 너비, 수직 방향 최대 0.80칸 높이로 표시한다. 방향·위치는 기존 게임의 실제 이동 상태를 따른다.
- 정식 게임 84개 수로의 입체 시점과 `/compare/`에서 공통 사용한다. `SHARK_ART`가 입체 시점에서 선택된 기본/항해사 스킨의 네 방향만 필요 시 디코딩한다. 원본 2장과 WebP는 생성 결과이며 별도 외부 다운로드가 없다.

## 최종 생성 프롬프트

기본 상어, 참조 2장(기본 portrait / swim), `transparent_background: true`:

> Create a production game sprite atlas, exactly FOUR poses of the SAME blue baby shark in the reference images, preserving the same adorable round blue/ivory body, huge friendly blue eyes, small smiling mouth, fins and forked tail. Transparent background. Square 2x2 grid of equal quadrants, each shark completely within its own quadrant with plenty of empty transparent gutter, no text or grid lines. Fixed orthographic camera 50 degrees above the horizontal water plane, viewer is south of the board looking north. This is a shallow 2.5D mobile puzzle camera, not strict overhead. Four cardinal world directions: TOP LEFT shark swims EAST, nose to image RIGHT, side face and white belly clearly visible, tail left. TOP RIGHT swims WEST, nose image LEFT, tail right. BOTTOM LEFT swims SOUTH TOWARDS camera, smiling face front, tail behind towards image TOP. BOTTOM RIGHT swims NORTH AWAY from camera, back of head and tail visible, tail towards image BOTTOM. Each pose is independently rendered in this same fixed camera, NOT a rotated image. All sharks lie horizontally swimming flat on the water plane, none standing upright. Matching proportions, world size, and light from upper left across all four. Stylized high quality soft 3D toy rendering, clean bold silhouette, vivid medium blue back with pale ivory underside, strong separation of face and fins. These must read at just 48 pixels on a turquoise canal, so favor a big expressive head and simplified details. No ground, no water, no cast shadow, no extra objects, no labels, no panels, no background. Use first reference for face and volume, second for body and fin identity; change camera and directional pose as specified.

항해사, 참조 3장(등대 portrait / swim + 위 기본 원본), `transparent_background: true`:

> Create ONE transparent production game sprite atlas with exactly FOUR directional poses of the sailor lighthouse baby shark. First two images are the REQUIRED character skin identity: pearl ivory body, broad vermilion red back bands, navy fins and tail, navy sailor cap with ivory brim and tiny golden anchor, small amber lantern at the collar. Preserve this specific skin. Third reference is the required 2x2 pose arrangement, camera, big expressive eyes, playful round body and soft 3D rendering quality. Copy the four poses and layout of the third image using the sailor skin from first two images. TOP LEFT EAST swimming head to RIGHT, TOP RIGHT WEST swimming head to LEFT, BOTTOM LEFT SOUTH swimming TOWARDS viewer with face visible and tail behind toward TOP, BOTTOM RIGHT NORTH swimming AWAY from viewer with tail toward BOTTOM. Same fixed orthographic camera roughly 50 degrees above horizontal water plane, looking north from south. All swimming horizontally, never upright. Each pose independently rendered, not rotated pictures. Navy cap stays on back of head, does not hide eyes; red bands on rear body remain large and easy to read. Each whole character fully inside its own equal quadrant, empty transparent gutters between characters and around margins. Use a slightly more compact body than reference 3, round huge head and little tail suitable for a one-cell mobile puzzle character. This must remain readable at 48 pixels, so simplify texture and prioritize face, navy hat, red bands. Matching world scale and upper-left illumination on all poses. No ground, water, scene, frame, labels, letters or drop shadows. Output only the 2x2 transparent atlas.
